What changes, and why

Illustrative data. Nothing here is built yet: this is the proposal, for you to approve or change before any code. Each point answers the auditor's QA-10 and your Part 4 item 9.

  1. You can read it. The panel is a solid surface in both themes; the lead page no longer shows through it. Today it uses the CRM's translucent card surface, and its header colour comes from a colour name that does not exist.
  2. End call is always there. A solid red button in the panel's header, which stays put while the panel scrolls; on a phone, a full-width bar at the bottom, within thumb reach. Today it is white text on a white button: the "blank white rectangle".
  3. Start call does what it says. It places the call through the dialler (CUBE), after the consent checks the server already makes, and the panel says what happened: your phone is ringing; it can't call, and why; or, on labs, where the dialler isn't connected, a call clearly marked as simulated. A number your role sees masked stays masked.
  4. One product in focus. The one the call is about (the card you started from, or else the latest in progress), with its pitch, facts and brochure. The others are a count and a click away. Twelve products no longer push everything off-screen.
  5. Actions that do something, and say what they did. Each acts on the spot and is listed under "Done on this call". One that doesn't apply is hidden (Mark Warm on a product already past Warm) or shown greyed out with the reason (an SMS opt-out). Today they are placeholders: "In this build they are indicative".
  6. Notes as you talk. Typed during the call, carried into the wrap-up, and used by the AI to draft the disposition. Sample transcripts appear only on a simulated call, marked as demo material.
  7. Beside the lead, not on top of it. On a laptop the panel docks to the right, so the lead page stays readable during the call, and the three columns that repeated the lead page shrink to a short "At a glance". On a phone it is a full-screen sheet.
  8. Nothing is lost by closing. After End call the panel stays until the call is saved, with or without the AI; the wrap-up cannot be dismissed by accident. What you did during the call is saved as you do it.
