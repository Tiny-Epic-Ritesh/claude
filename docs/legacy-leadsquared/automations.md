## CRITICAL FINDINGS FOR THE NEW CRM

**1. Volume is enormous and concentrated in mirroring/scoring.**
Two automations alone (`Add Activity on Opportunity as per Lead` at 14.1M and
`Activity Score` at 8.0M) account for the majority of all automation executions.
Both are *data-propagation* jobs — copying activity from Lead to Opportunity and
recomputing a score. In a properly normalised model neither would need to exist:
activities would attach to a shared party/timeline, and score would be a computed
projection, not a stamped field. **This is a direct argument for a shared timeline
entity and derived/materialised fields in the new CRM.**

**2. "Capture X Date" automations are compensating for a missing platform feature.**
There are at least six automations whose entire job is to stamp a timestamp when a
field changes (Created Date, PAN Submitted Date, MQL Date, RTT Date, First Intent).
LeadSquared has no native "field history / stage-entry timestamp", so every date
someone wants to report on has to be manufactured by an automation writing into
an `mx_` field. **The new CRM should have first-class field-change history and
stage-entry/exit timestamps built in.** That removes ~6 automations and ~15 fields.

**3. "- Clone" suffix appears 5+ times.**
Lead Update Automation ... - Clone; When Opportunity Type Is Sales Support ... - Clone;
Global Investment Opportunity -30 Dec - Clone; WhatsApp to Lead source Facebook ... - Clone;
Mobileapp1 leads on prospects - Clone. Cloning is being used instead of
parameterisation/versioning. There's no way to tell what differs between an
original and its clone without opening both. **New CRM needs versioned, diffable
automation definitions.**

**4. Date-stamped names are the de facto version-control system.**
"22April2025", "23 March 2026", "9th March'26", "19Aug 2025V4-", "-30Dec2023".
Admins encode the deploy date in the name because the platform gives them no
change history. Same root cause as #3.

**5. Dead and orphaned automations are live.**
`Mobileapp1 leads on prospects - Clone` (0 triggers, published 07/2026),
`Welcome Email on lead creation for Partner Onboarding` (1 trigger since 10/2025),
`Automation_231` (default unnamed), `60001589 No Cross-Sell` (a bare account
number as a name). No lifecycle/retirement process.

**6. Multiple automations write to the same fields on the same trigger.**
At least three "Lead Updated" automations (`Lead Update Automation 20-03-2026`,
`Lead Update Automation 9th March'26 - RTT Leads Excluded - Clone`,
`New Automation for RTT Leads`) fire on the same event against overlapping
populations. Execution order is not guaranteed or documented → this is a live
race-condition risk in the current system and must not be reproduced.
**New CRM needs explicit rule ordering/priority and conflict detection.**

**7. Person-named and campaign-named automations.**
"Leads On System and Manjushri's Id", "Partner Acqusition- Richa Camp"
(also a typo: Acqusition). Business logic bound to an individual's identity is a
continuity risk when that person leaves.
